`@cap-js/sqlite` 2.4.3, `@cap-js/db-service` 2.11.3, `@cap-js/cds-test` 1.0.2 (all Apache-2.0) and `better-sqlite3` 13.0.3 (MIT) were installed with `--no-save` for a runtime probe that the sandbox blocked.
